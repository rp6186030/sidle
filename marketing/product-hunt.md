# Product Hunt

**Name:** Sidle

**Tagline** (60 chars): Volume, brightness & seek on your Mac trackpad edges

**Topics:** Mac, Productivity, Open Source, Menu Bar Apps

**Description** (260 chars):

Sidle turns the edges of your Mac trackpad into controls. Press the right edge and slide for volume, left for brightness, bottom to scrub video. Click a top corner to launch apps or run Shortcuts. Free and open source, no account.

**Gallery:** 1) demo video, 2) GIF of volume slide with hand visible, 3) GIF of seek preview, 4) Corners settings screenshot, 5) Gestures settings screenshot.

**Maker's first comment:**

Hey Product Hunt 👋

My hand lives on the trackpad, but volume and brightness live on the keyboard. Sidle fixes that: the thin strip along each edge becomes a slider, and the top corners become buttons.

What I cared about most was that it never fires by accident. You have to rest one finger on the edge and physically click before anything happens. Each step ticks under your finger, and the pointer stays exactly where it was.

It is free and the source is on GitHub. Because it is free I have not paid Apple for notarization, so the first launch needs one extra step, explained on the page.

I would love to know: what would you put on a corner double-click?
