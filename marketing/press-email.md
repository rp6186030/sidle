**Subject:** Free open-source Mac app: volume and brightness on the trackpad edges

Hi {name},

I read your piece on {their related article} and thought this might be up your street.

Sidle is a free, open-source menu bar app that turns the edges of the Mac trackpad into controls: press the right edge and slide for volume, the left for brightness, the bottom to scrub a video. The top corners act as buttons for launching apps or running Shortcuts.

Two things make it different from the paid apps in this space. It is MIT licensed with the full source on GitHub, and it only triggers on a deliberate click on the edge, so normal trackpad use is unaffected.
