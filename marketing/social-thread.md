# X / Mastodon / Bluesky thread

1/ Your Mac trackpad has four edges you never use.

I made Sidle so they control volume, brightness and video scrubbing. Free and open source. [demo video]

2/ Right edge: click and slide for volume.
Left edge: brightness.
You get the normal macOS indicator and a haptic tick on every step. [GIF]

3/ Bottom edge: slide sideways, see "+15 s", release to jump. Works in YouTube, Netflix, QuickTime, Music. [GIF]

4/ Top corners are buttons. One to four clicks each: mute, open an app, press any hotkey, run a Shortcut. [GIF]

5/ It never fires by accident. A gesture needs one finger resting on the edge plus a physical click. Palms and scrolling do nothing.
