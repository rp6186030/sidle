# Sidle launch kit

Positioning: **Sidle turns the edges of your Mac trackpad into volume, brightness and seek controls, and the corners into buttons. Free, open source, no account, no license key.**

Why someone picks Sidle over the alternatives:

| | Sidle | MacEdge | Slidr |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Free, open source | $3 | $4.99 or Setapp |
| Source available | Yes (MIT) | No | No |
| Corner actions | 1–4 clicks, apps/links/hotkeys/Shortcuts | 1–4 clicks | Not a focus |
| Notarized | No (one Terminal command) | Yes | Yes |

Be upfront about the last row everywhere. On Hacker News and Reddit, "not notarized because I won't pay $99/year for a free app, here is the source" lands better than hiding it.
